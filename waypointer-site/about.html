About

Every Business Has a Why.
Ours Is the Kingdom.

Why We Exist

The Waypointer Collective was built on a simple conviction: commerce is not spiritually neutral. Every business serves something, and every purchase advances someone's agenda. Followers of Jesus should have a way to align their spending with their faith. Kingdom-minded businesses deserve to be found. The Waypointer Collective brings them together.

What We Are

The Waypointer Collective is a curated collection of businesses that put Jesus at the center. Every member is vetted and accountable — actively, verifiably advancing the Gospel through what they do. Consumers can browse with confidence, knowing every listing represents a business doing real Kingdom work.

The Standard

Any business can call itself Christian, or can be owned by a Christian. We require more than that.

Every member demonstrates measurable Gospel advancement through financial giving to Gospel-advancing causes, ministry programs, or documented community impact that brings attention to Jesus. Membership is reviewed annually. Businesses that no longer meet the standard are removed. This is what makes the Collective different: it's a community with a bar.

The Vision

The Waypointer Collective is more than a directory. We're building toward a future where Kingdom-minded businesses can find each other, serve each other, and together advance the Gospel through commerce. We are creating space for consumers to find what they need while moving the Gospel forward.

The mission is simple: Your spending. His Kingdom.

A Note to Business Owners

If you're reading this and wondering whether your business belongs here, start with the question we ask every applicant: How does your company actively forward the Gospel? If you have a real answer, we want to hear from you.
