For Consumers

The Waypointer Collective is a curated collection of businesses that put Jesus at the center. Every member is vetted and accountable, actively, verifiably advancing the Gospel through what they do. We exist to connect consumers who want their spending to advance the Gospel with businesses that are already doing it.
Every business in the Collective has been reviewed by our leadership and must demonstrate measurable Gospel advancement through financial giving, ministry programs, or documented community impact. Membership is also reviewed annually. Businesses that no longer meet the standard are removed.
The Collective is built for followers of Jesus, but the door is open to anyone. If you're curious about what it looks like for a business to put Jesus at the center, you're welcome here. If you just want to purchase great products or services, you're welcome here.

For Business Owners

Any business or organization that actively, verifiably advances the Gospel through its commercial activity. This includes LLCs, S-Corps, sole proprietors, partnerships, and 501(c)(3) organizations engaged in selling goods or services. Donation-only organizations are not eligible.
The Waypointer Collective is specifically for businesses whose work actively spreads the message of Jesus Christ — not just businesses that do good in their community. Good deeds matter, but they don't meet our standard on their own. A business that feeds the homeless, serves veterans, or donates to a local charity is doing meaningful work. But unless the Gospel of Jesus is explicitly being shared in the process, it doesn't qualify for the Collective.

Examples that qualify:
  • Donating a percentage of sales to Bible translation, church planting, or evangelistic organizations
  • Employing and discipling staff as an intentional ministry practice
  • Incorporating Gospel witness directly into your business model — sharing who Jesus is with customers, including Gospel materials with orders, etc.
  • Operating a ministry program connected to the business that explicitly proclaims the Gospel
Examples that don't qualify:
  • Charitable giving to causes that serve people but don't share the Gospel
  • Community service or humanitarian work without explicit Gospel proclamation
  • A faith-inspired business culture with no measurable Gospel-advancing component
Not sure if your business qualifies? Reach out to us at hello@thewaypointercollective.com — we're happy to talk it through before you apply.
