Frequently Asked Questions

For Consumers

The Waypointer Collective is a curated collection of businesses that put Jesus at the center. Every member is vetted and accountable, actively, verifiably advancing the Gospel through what they do. We exist to connect consumers who want their spending to advance the Gospel with businesses that are already doing it.
Every business in the Collective has been reviewed by our leadership and must demonstrate measurable Gospel advancement through financial giving, ministry programs, or documented community impact. Membership is also reviewed annually. Businesses that no longer meet the standard are removed.
The Collective is built for followers of Jesus, but the door is open to anyone. If you're curious about what it looks like for a business to put Jesus at the center, you're welcome here. If you just want to purchase great products or services, you're welcome here.

For Business Owners

Any business or organization that actively, verifiably advances the Gospel through its commercial activity. This includes LLCs, S-Corps, sole proprietors, partnerships, and 501(c)(3) organizations engaged in selling goods or services. Donation-only organizations are not eligible.
Every application is reviewed by The Waypointer Collective leadership using a mixed quantitative and qualitative process. We evaluate your answers to our Gospel advancement questions and the proof you submit.
You'll receive a response within 5 business days of submitting your application.
